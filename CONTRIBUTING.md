# Contributing to HeatherDB

Code, documentation, examples, benchmarks and reproducible reports are welcome.
Every pull request is reviewed manually by a maintainer before merging.

AI-assisted contributions are welcome. You must understand and verify the work,
document its behaviour and limitations, and provide the checks you actually
ran. Briefly describe how AI was used and what you independently verified.
Unreviewed generated patches and unsupported claims are not ready for review.

Read the [contributor guide](docs/how-to/contribute.md) for the workflow,
validation requirements and pull request checklist, or
[read it on the website](https://aphorion.co/heatherdb/docs/how-to/contribute/).
